# Block P Neon Sign

A wall-mounted, 3D-printed keepsake of four years in the Purdue All-American Marching Band, shaped like the band's Block P formation. An 8" screen fills the opening of the P and plays show videos and photos. A thin gold LED outline runs around the edge, and speakers fire down from under the screen. It runs on a Raspberry Pi 4B and is controlled from a computer or phone on the home network.
