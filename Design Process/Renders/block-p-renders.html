Power wiring

One cord comes in at 12V, and the converter inside the sign makes a steady 5V. The Pi and the LEDs each take their own branch, so the LEDs never draw through the Pi.

Bring-up order

  1. Check that the adapter is center positive, then connect the jack and converter.
  2. Measure the converter output with nothing else connected. Expect about 5.0 to 5.25V.
  3. Solder the 5V junction and the Pi's USB-C lead, then boot the Pi.
  5. Play a video and run vcgencmd get_throttled. A result of 0x0 means no low-voltage events.
  6. Add the strip's power at both ends and the capacitor, then the level shifter, resistor and data line.
  7. Run everything together for a few hours before it goes in the enclosure.
